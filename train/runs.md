SmolVLA (cameras `top,wrist,inspect`, the `perfect` string): finite actions in the dataset's units (normalized, like the whole red-lego set, D29), 3 chunks at about 7.4 s each on this CPU, 150 steps in 26.8 s, so on a CPU the arm would stop ~7 s every 1.7 s of motion.
